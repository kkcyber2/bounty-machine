# Acme AI Assistant — Bug Bounty Program

Platform: HackerOne

We welcome security research on our AI assistant. Please read the scope and
rules carefully before testing.

## In Scope

- https://api.acme.com
- chat.acme.com
- The `acme-assistant` mobile app backend

## Out of Scope

- admin.acme.com
- Any denial-of-service or rate-limit exhaustion testing
- Social engineering of Acme staff or customers
- Physical attacks against Acme premises
- Reports that only demonstrate a jailbreak without a concrete impact

## Rules

- Please do not exceed 2 requests per second. Maximum 300 requests per test.
- Use a dedicated test account. Test accounts are available on request.
- Automated scanners are permitted only against in-scope assets at low rate.
- Safe harbour applies to all testing performed in accordance with this policy.
- We operate a 90-day disclosure embargo.

## Reward Ranges

| Severity | Range |
|----------|-------|
| Critical | $4,000 - $7,000 |
| High     | $1,500 - $3,000 |
| Medium   | $500 - $1,200 |
| Low      | $100 - $400 |
